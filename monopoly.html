Classic rules

Roll two dice and move. Doubles earn another roll; three in a row sends you to Jail. Buy unowned property or send it to auction. Pay rent when you land on someone else's property. Complete a colour set to build houses and hotels. Pass GO to collect $200. Mortgages raise cash at half the price. Bankruptcy transfers your assets to your creditor; last player standing wins.
